Business Terms

Business Glossary

Plain-English definitions of common business and legal terms.

LLC
Limited Liability Company — a business structure that protects owners' personal assets from business debts and liabilities while offering tax flexibility.
EIN
Employer Identification Number — a federal tax ID number issued by the IRS. Required to open a business bank account, hire employees, and file business taxes.
DBA
Doing Business As — a fictitious name used by a business that differs from the legal registered name. Allows you to operate under a different brand name.
Operating Agreement
A document outlining the ownership structure, management, and operating procedures of an LLC. Highly recommended even for single-member LLCs.
Articles of Incorporation
The foundational document filed with the state to legally form a corporation. Also called a Certificate of Organization for LLCs in Pennsylvania.
MBE
Minority Business Enterprise — certification for businesses at least 51% owned by a minority individual or group. Opens set-aside procurement opportunities.
DBE
Disadvantaged Business Enterprise — a federal certification for socially and economically disadvantaged business owners, primarily used in transportation contracting.
SAM.gov
System for Award Management — the federal database where businesses must register to receive federal contracts, grants, and other awards.
CDFI
Community Development Financial Institution — mission-driven lenders that provide financial services to underserved markets and businesses that may not qualify for traditional bank loans.
Cash Flow
The movement of money in and out of your business. Positive cash flow means more money is coming in than going out. Managing cash flow is critical to business survival.
Gross Margin
Revenue minus the cost of goods sold, expressed as a percentage. Indicates how much profit a business retains after direct production costs.
Market Assessment
An analysis of a business's target market, including market size, customer demographics, competitive landscape, and demand for a product or service.
