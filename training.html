Cohort Program

The PluggedInBC Entrepreneur Cohort

Our flagship cohort brings together 8–12 aspiring entrepreneurs for an intensive 6-week journey through the fundamentals of starting a business — combining expert instruction with peer learning and accountability.

Held quarterly, the cohort covers everything in Phase 1 of the Enterprise Pathway in a structured, supportive group format. Graduates leave with a registered business, a business plan, and a network of fellow entrepreneurs.

📅 Next Cohort: June 2025  |  Applications open April 1, 2025  |  Free

Apply Now

6-Week Curriculum

1 Business concept validation & market research
2 Business planning fundamentals
3 Legal structure & business formation
4 Financial basics & cash flow
5 Marketing strategy & brand development
6 Launch preparation & client acquisition
