About Us

About PluggedInBC

Building the entrepreneurship infrastructure Beaver County has always deserved.

Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

PluggedInBC advances economic equity in Beaver County by equipping historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs with the technical assistance, business skills, and capital access strategies needed to launch, sustain, and scale competitive businesses. As a regional Business Assistance Service Center, we transform economic potential into enterprise — bridging the gap between underserved communities and lasting economic mobility.

Our Vision

A Beaver County where historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs have full access to the tools, capital, and networks needed to build sustainable, generational businesses.

Our History

Our Story

PluggedInBC was founded to close the opportunity gap in Beaver County. What began as a technology training and workforce development organization evolved through years of direct community engagement into something deeper: a recognition that economic mobility requires more than job placement — it requires ownership.

We listened to community members who had been overlooked by traditional business support systems. We heard from aspiring entrepreneurs who had the drive, the talent, and the ideas — but lacked access to professional-grade guidance, capital networks, and the technical knowledge to navigate the business formation process.

Today, PluggedInBC operates as Beaver County's regional Business Assistance Service Center, formally aligned with Pennsylvania's Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance Program, delivering professional-grade entrepreneurship support to the communities that need it most.

HDBA Program Affiliation

PluggedInBC operates as a certified Business Assistance Service Center under the Pennsylvania Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance (HDBA) Program, administered by the PA Department of Community and Economic Development (DCED). Our services are designed to meet and exceed HDBA program requirements for business counseling, technical assistance, and entrepreneur development. Through this affiliation, we are able to deliver high-quality, no-cost services to qualifying historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs throughout Beaver County.
