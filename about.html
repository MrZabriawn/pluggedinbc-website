Who We Are

About PluggedInBC

We are Beaver County's Business Assistance Service Center — connecting historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs to the skills, support, and capital they need to succeed.

Our Mission

Advancing Economic Equity, One Business at a Time

"PluggedInBC advances economic equity in Beaver County by equipping historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs with the technical assistance, business skills, and capital access strategies needed to launch, sustain, and scale competitive businesses."

We believe great business ideas exist in every community — and that the difference between a dream and a business is often access: access to knowledge, to networks, and to capital. We exist to close that gap permanently.

Every service we offer, every workshop we deliver, and every partnership we build is in service of one goal: more thriving, sustainable businesses owned by people from Beaver County's historically disadvantaged communities.

🏛️ HDBA Authorization

PluggedInBC is an authorized Business Assistance Service Center under Pennsylvania's Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance (HDBA) Program, administered by the PA Department of Community and Economic Development. All services are provided to eligible clients at no cost.

📍 Our Service Area

Based in Beaver Falls, Pennsylvania, we serve entrepreneurs across all of Beaver County — including Aliquippa, Monaca, Ambridge, Conway, Ellwood City, New Brighton, and surrounding communities.

✅ Who We Serve

We serve aspiring and existing business owners who are historically disadvantaged, including minority-owned, women-owned, veteran-owned, and other underserved entrepreneurs in Beaver County.

Our Story

From Technology Training to Business Transformation

PluggedInBC was born in Beaver Falls with a clear-eyed understanding of economic reality. For too long, Beaver County's historically disadvantaged communities have faced systemic barriers to entrepreneurship — lack of startup capital, limited access to mentorship, insufficient knowledge of legal and financial systems, and an ecosystem not designed with them in mind.

We started with technology training and workforce development, helping community members gain the digital skills and credentials they needed to compete in a changing economy. But we quickly saw that our clients had bigger ambitions: they wanted to build businesses, not just find jobs. They wanted to be employers, not just employees.

That insight shaped everything. We evolved our model, deepened our partnerships, and formally became an authorized center under Pennsylvania's Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance Program. Today, PluggedInBC is a full-service business assistance center — offering one-on-one counseling, business formation support, financial management guidance, marketing strategy, procurement assistance, and direct connections to capital.

We are not a charity. We are an economic development engine, rooted in the community we serve. Our clients are business owners — and we treat them that way from day one.

🌟 Our Growth Milestone

Since becoming an authorized HDBA Business Assistance Service Center, PluggedInBC has served 75+ clients, helped launch 20+ businesses, facilitated access to $250,000+ in capital, and delivered 300+ hours of one-on-one counseling across Beaver County.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't buzzwords — they're the principles that guide every interaction, every program, and every decision we make.

⚖️

Equity

We recognize that equal access requires removing unequal barriers. Our programs are designed to level the playing field for historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs — meeting people where they are, not where we assume they should be.

💪

Empowerment

We don't just hand people resources — we help them build the skills, knowledge, and confidence to operate independently and successfully. Every service we provide builds lasting capability, not dependency.

🏆

Excellence

Our clients deserve the same quality of business support available anywhere in the region. We hold ourselves to high professional standards in counseling quality, program design, and partnership development.

🏘️

Community

We are rooted in Beaver County and accountable to the communities we serve. Our success is measured by the vitality of local neighborhoods — not just business metrics or grant reports.

📊

Accountability

We track outcomes, report transparently, and continuously improve our programming. Our funders, partners, and clients deserve honest accounting of what we accomplish — and what we need to do better.

✨

Innovation

The needs of entrepreneurs are constantly evolving. We continuously improve our programs, partnerships, and approaches to ensure we're delivering the most relevant and effective support possible.
