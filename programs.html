Our Programs

The Enterprise Pathway

Structured pathways from first idea to sustainable growth — built for Beaver County entrepreneurs.

Start the Pathway Apply for Cohort
Program Model

A Structured Journey to Sustainable Business

The Enterprise Pathway meets entrepreneurs where they are and guides them where they need to go.

1

Phase 1

Idea & Formation

Validate your concept, write your plan, form your business, and get registered.

2

Phase 2

Launch & Operations

Build your brand, manage your finances, access capital, and get your business running.

3

Phase 3

Growth & Scaling

Win government contracts, scale your operations, and build lasting wealth.

Phase Details

What Each Phase Includes

1

Phase 1

Idea & Formation

Who this serves: Aspiring entrepreneurs, workforce program graduates, community residents with a business concept.

Services Included

  • Initial Business Assessment
  • Business Concept Validation
  • Business Plan Development
  • Legal Structure Guidance
  • Business Formation Assistance
  • Market Assessment
  • Registration Assistance
  • Employer Responsibility Training

🎯 Phase 1 Milestone: By the end of Phase 1, you have a registered business entity, an EIN, a foundational business plan, and a clear understanding of your market.

2

Phase 2

Launch & Operations

Who this serves: Newly formed businesses, micro-enterprises, early-stage startups (0–18 months in operation).

Services Included

  • Marketing Strategy Development
  • Brand Identity & Digital Presence
  • Accounting & Financial Management
  • Cash Flow Projection & Budgeting
  • Tax Compliance Consultation
  • Loan & Grant Application Packaging
  • Capital Source Referrals
  • Strategic Planning

🎯 Phase 2 Milestone: By the end of Phase 2, your business is operating, has a marketing presence, understands its financials, and has applied for at least one capital or contract opportunity.

3

Phase 3

Growth & Scaling

Who this serves: Operating businesses seeking revenue diversification, contract procurement, or expansion into new markets.

Services Included

  • Contract Procurement Support
  • Government Contracting Database Registration
  • Supplier Diversity Certification Assistance
  • Strategic Partnerships & Referrals
  • Advanced Financial Management
  • Expansion & Scaling Planning
  • Peer Learning Cohorts

🎯 Phase 3 Milestone: By the end of Phase 3, your business has diversified revenue, active contract bids or awards, and a documented growth plan.

Cohort Program

The PluggedInBC Entrepreneur Cohort

Our flagship cohort-based learning experience brings together a small group of aspiring entrepreneurs for an intensive six-week journey through the fundamentals of starting a business.

The cohort model creates accountability, peer learning, and a supportive community of fellow entrepreneurs — all while receiving expert guidance from our counselors.

Duration
6 Weeks
Frequency
Quarterly
Class Size
8–12 Participants
Cost
Free
Apply for the Next Cohort

What You'll Cover

  • Business concept validation and market research
  • Business planning fundamentals
  • Legal structure and business formation
  • Financial basics and cash flow
  • Marketing strategy and brand development
  • Launch preparation and client acquisition
