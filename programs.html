Phase Details

What You'll Get at Each Stage

Phase 1  |  Pre-Launch

🌱 Idea & Formation

Who it's for: Aspiring entrepreneurs with a business idea who haven't yet launched — or people still exploring entrepreneurship as a path.

What we help with:

  • Business idea validation and market assessment
  • Business plan development (full plan or lean canvas)
  • Legal structure selection (LLC, sole proprietorship, corporation)
  • Pennsylvania business registration and licensing
  • EIN application and business bank account setup
  • Initial financial projections and startup budget
  • Introduction to funding options for startup-stage businesses
Start Here →
Phase 2  |  0 – 18 Months in Business

🚀 Launch & Operations

Who it's for: Entrepreneurs who have recently launched or are in their first year of operations — building systems, finding customers, and establishing themselves in the market.

What we help with:

  • Ongoing one-on-one business counseling
  • Accounting setup and bookkeeping basics
  • Cash flow management and projections
  • Marketing strategy and customer acquisition
  • Employer responsibility training (if hiring)
  • Tax compliance consultation
  • Strategic planning for growth
  • Introduction to procurement and government contracting
View All Services →
Phase 3  |  18+ Months in Business

📈 Growth & Scaling

Who it's for: Established business owners ready to scale — pursue contracts, access capital, build a team, and grow market share.

What we help with:

  • Advanced strategic planning and scaling roadmap
  • Government contracting and procurement support
  • SAM.gov and PA Supplier Portal registration
  • Supplier diversity certifications (MBE, WBE, DBE, SDB)
  • SBA loan and CDFI microloan application packaging
  • DCED grant application assistance
  • Advanced financial management and analysis
  • Hiring, HR basics, and employer compliance

Cohort Program

Learn Together. Grow Together.

Our flagship cohort program brings entrepreneurs together for an intensive, structured business development experience.

Enterprise Cohort — Quarterly Program

The PluggedInBC Enterprise Cohort is a 6–8 week intensive program for aspiring and early-stage entrepreneurs. Participants move through curriculum together, building business plans, financial projections, and marketing strategies in a structured group setting — with individual coaching woven throughout.

Next Cohort: Applications open for Summer 2026. Limited spots available.

Format: Weekly sessions (in-person and virtual options) plus individual counseling appointments.

Who Should Apply: Aspiring entrepreneurs in Beaver County who are ready to commit 6–8 weeks to intensive business development work.

Apply for Next Cohort

What's Included in the Cohort

W1

Business Idea Validation

Market research basics, identifying your target customer, and validating demand for your product or service.

W2

Business Plan Development

Building your complete business plan — executive summary, market analysis, operations plan, and financial projections.

W3

Legal Structure & Registration

Choosing your legal entity, registering in Pennsylvania, EIN, licenses, permits, and business banking.

W4

Marketing & Brand Identity

Building your brand, digital presence, pricing strategy, and customer acquisition plan.

W5

Financial Management

Bookkeeping basics, cash flow, profit & loss, budgeting, and knowing your numbers.

W6

Capital Access & Pitch Prep

Funding landscape, loan applications, and pitching your business to lenders and investors.
