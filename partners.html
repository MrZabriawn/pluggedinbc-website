Our Network

Our Partners

A regional ecosystem built for entrepreneurship — connecting Beaver County entrepreneurs to the resources, capital, and expertise they need.

Partner Network

Organizations We Partner With

PluggedInBC does not work alone. Our network of partners amplifies our reach and deepens our impact.

🏛️ Government Partners

State Agency

PA Department of Community and Economic Development (DCED)

HDBA Program funder and oversight body. The PA DCED administers the Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance Program that enables PluggedInBC to deliver no-cost services to qualifying entrepreneurs.

County Government

Beaver County Commissioners

County-level economic development collaboration and community partnership. Working together to strengthen entrepreneurship infrastructure across Beaver County's municipalities.

Federal Agency

SBA Pittsburgh District Office

Federal small business programs and referrals. The SBA Pittsburgh District Office connects PluggedInBC clients to SBA loan programs, contracting programs, and federal resources.

📚 Training & Technical Assistance Partners

SBDC Partner

Beaver County Small Business Development Center (SBDC)

Co-counseling and training collaboration. The Beaver County SBDC and PluggedInBC work together to ensure comprehensive coverage of business development needs across the county.

Educational Institution

Community College of Beaver County

Training pipeline and credential pathways. CCBC provides workforce and continuing education programs that feed into PluggedInBC's entrepreneurship pipeline.

Mentoring Network

Pittsburgh SCORE Chapter

Mentor referrals and specialized expertise. SCORE's network of experienced volunteer mentors provides PluggedInBC clients with additional guidance in specialized areas.

💰 Capital Partners

Mission Lenders

CDFIs — Community Development Financial Institutions

Alternative lending for underserved businesses. CDFI partners offer flexible loan products designed for businesses that may not qualify for traditional bank financing.

Local Banking

First Keystone Community Bank

Local lending relationships and small business financial services. A community banking partner committed to small business development in the Beaver County region.

State Capital

PA DCED Capital Programs

State-level funding programs including low-interest loan programs, equity investments, and other financial assistance for Pennsylvania small businesses.

🤝 Community Partners

Business Network

Beaver County Chamber of Commerce

Business network and peer connections. The Chamber provides PluggedInBC clients with access to a broader business community and networking opportunities.

Municipal Government

Beaver Falls City Government

Local community partnerships and outreach. Working with Beaver Falls city government to reach entrepreneurs in our home community and support local economic development.

Community Organizations

Faith & Community Organizations

Outreach and referral network. Faith communities and community organizations serve as trusted messengers and referral sources connecting residents to PluggedInBC services.
