Who We Are

Beaver County's Business Development Engine

For years, PluggedInBC has been at the intersection of economic opportunity and underserved communities in Beaver County. Today, we're evolving into the region's first dedicated Business Assistance Service Center for historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs — operating under Pennsylvania's HDBA Program to deliver the professional-grade services our community deserves.

  • Free one-on-one business counseling
