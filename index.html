Who We Are

Built for Beaver County. Built for You.

PluggedInBC is an authorized Business Assistance Service Center under Pennsylvania's Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance (HDBA) Program, administered by the PA Department of Community and Economic Development.

We were founded in Beaver Falls with a simple belief: access to opportunity shouldn't depend on who you know or how much money you already have. Every entrepreneur deserves a real shot at success.

Learn About Us

🎯 Our Mission

Advancing economic equity in Beaver County by equipping historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs with technical assistance, business skills, and capital access strategies.

📍 Serving Beaver County

Based in Beaver Falls, PA — serving entrepreneurs in Aliquippa, Monaca, Ambridge, Conway, Ellwood City, and communities across Beaver County.

✅ Free Services

All PluggedInBC services are provided at no cost to eligible clients through the HDBA Program.
