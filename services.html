Business Assistance Services

Our Full Suite of Services

Comprehensive, no-cost business assistance services for historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs in Beaver County — from startup to scale.

HDBA Program

These services are provided as part of Pennsylvania's Historically Disadvantaged Business Assistance (HDBA) Program, administered by the PA Department of Community and Economic Development. All services are available at no cost to eligible clients.

Complete Service Menu

14 Core Services. All Free. All Professional.

Every service we provide is designed to give historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs the same quality of support available anywhere in the market.

🤝

One-on-One Business Counseling

Confidential, individualized counseling sessions with experienced business advisors. We work with you to diagnose challenges, identify opportunities, set priorities, and build a clear action plan. Counseling is the backbone of all our services and available at any stage of your business journey.

Schedule a Consultation
📄

Business Plan Development

A professionally developed business plan is your roadmap and your most important tool for accessing capital. Our advisors guide you through every section — executive summary, market analysis, organizational structure, products and services, marketing strategy, and financial projections — producing a plan lenders and investors can act on.

Schedule a Consultation
🏢

Business Formation Assistance

Choosing and establishing the right legal structure is foundational. We help you understand the differences between sole proprietorship, LLC, S-Corp, and C-Corp — considering liability, tax implications, and your business goals. We then support you through Pennsylvania registration, EIN application, operating agreements, and business banking setup.

Schedule a Consultation
🔍

Market Assessment

Before investing time and money, you need to know your market. We conduct structured market assessments that identify your target customers, analyze competitors, evaluate market size, and validate that real demand exists for your product or service. This critical step prevents costly mistakes and sharpens your business model.

Schedule a Consultation
📣

Marketing Strategy Guidance

Your business needs customers. We help you build a practical marketing strategy tailored to your budget, industry, and target market — covering brand identity, digital marketing, social media, community outreach, email marketing, and customer retention. No vague advice — only actionable plans you can execute.

Schedule a Consultation
🗺️

Strategic Planning

Growth doesn't happen by accident. Our strategic planning service helps you define a 1-to-3-year vision for your business, set measurable goals, identify the resources and milestones needed to achieve them, and build accountability systems to stay on track. Ideal for businesses in Phase 2 and 3 of the Enterprise Pathway.

Schedule a Consultation
📊

Accounting & Financial Management

Strong financial management is the foundation of a sustainable business. We provide guidance on bookkeeping systems, record-keeping best practices, profit and loss analysis, and financial decision-making. We help you understand your numbers so you can make confident, data-driven choices for your business.

Schedule a Consultation
💵

Cash Flow Projections

Cash flow is the lifeblood of any business — and the top reason small businesses fail. We help you build 12-to-24-month cash flow projections that reveal gaps before they become crises, support loan applications, and give you a realistic picture of your business's financial trajectory. Lenders require it. Smart owners want it.

Schedule a Consultation
🧾

Tax Compliance Consultation

Understanding your business tax obligations — quarterly estimated taxes, sales tax, payroll taxes, and deductions — is essential to avoiding costly penalties. Our advisors provide general tax compliance guidance and connect you to qualified tax professionals when your situation requires specialized expertise.

Schedule a Consultation
👔

Employer Responsibility Training

Ready to hire your first employee? This is a major milestone — and a major responsibility. We provide training on employer obligations including federal and state wage laws, workers' compensation, unemployment insurance, I-9 compliance, and basic HR practices. Know the rules before you make your first hire.

Schedule a Consultation
🏛️

Contract Procurement Support

Government contracts can be transformational for small businesses. We guide you through understanding the procurement process, identifying bid opportunities, reading solicitations, preparing competitive proposals, and building relationships with contracting officers. With trillions spent by government annually, your business deserves a seat at the table.

Schedule a Consultation
🌐

Government Database Registration

To do business with federal and state government, you must be properly registered. We assist with SAM.gov (System for Award Management) registration and renewal, the PA Supplier Portal, and any other database registrations required for your target contracting opportunities. This is Step 1 for any government contracting strategy.

Schedule a Consultation
📝

Loan & Grant Application Packaging

A strong application dramatically increases your chances of funding approval. We help you assemble complete, professional loan and grant application packages — including business plans, financial projections, personal financial statements, executive summaries, and all required attachments. We don't just point you to funding; we help you win it.

Schedule a Consultation
🏦

Referrals to Capital Sources

Access to capital is one of the most persistent barriers facing historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs. We maintain active relationships with SBA lenders, CDFI partners, community banks, credit unions, angel investors, and DCED grant programs — and make warm, targeted referrals to match your capital needs with the right source.

Schedule a Consultation

How to Access Our Services

1

Complete the Intake Form

Fill out our brief online intake form or call us directly. Tell us about where you are in your business journey and what you're trying to accomplish.

2

Schedule an Initial Consultation

A team member will contact you within 2 business days to schedule your free first appointment — available in-person, by phone, or virtually.

3

Build Your Action Plan

During your first session, we'll assess your needs, identify priorities, and build a customized action plan with clear next steps.

4

Receive Ongoing Support

Ongoing counseling sessions, workshop participation, and access to our full service suite — all at no cost to eligible clients.
