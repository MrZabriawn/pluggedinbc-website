HDBA Business Assistance Services

Business Assistance
Services

Professional-grade technical assistance. Free for qualifying entrepreneurs.

As a Business Assistance Service Center under Pennsylvania's HDBA Program, PluggedInBC delivers a comprehensive suite of technical assistance services designed to help historically disadvantaged entrepreneurs start, operate, and grow competitive businesses. All services are provided at no cost to qualifying clients.

What We Offer

Our Full Service Menu

12 core services covering every stage of business development.

🧭

One-on-One Business Counseling

Personalized sessions with an experienced business counselor covering your specific challenges, goals, and opportunities. Available in-person and virtually.

📄

Business Plan Development

We help you research, write, and refine a professional business plan — the foundation for funding, partnerships, and strategic clarity.

🏛️

Business Formation Assistance

From choosing your legal structure to filing articles of incorporation, obtaining your EIN, and drafting operating agreements — we walk you through every step.

🔍

Market Assessment

Understand your target market, competitive landscape, and positioning before you invest. Our counselors guide you through market research and feasibility analysis.

📣

Marketing Strategy

Build a marketing plan that works for your budget and goals — from digital presence and social media to community outreach and brand development.

🗺️

Strategic Planning

Facilitated strategic planning sessions to help you set goals, identify priorities, and build the roadmap your business needs to grow.

📊

Accounting & Financial Management

Learn the financial fundamentals every business owner needs: bookkeeping basics, reading financial statements, managing accounts receivable and payable.

💰

Cash Flow Projections

Work with our counselors to build cash flow projections and financial models that inform your decisions and satisfy lender requirements.

🧾

Tax Compliance Consultation

Understand your tax obligations as a business owner. We provide guidance and referrals to qualified tax professionals for complex situations.

👥

Employer Responsibility Training

Planning to hire? Learn what you need to know about payroll, workers' compensation, employment law, and HR basics.

🏛️

Contract Procurement Support

We help you identify, pursue, and compete for government and corporate contracts — from bid preparation to relationship building with procurement officers.

🏦

Loan & Grant Application Packaging

We help you identify funding sources, prepare application materials, and present your business in the strongest possible light to lenders and grantors.

How We Deliver

Flexible Service Delivery

We meet you where you are — in-person, online, or in a group setting.

🏢

In-Person Counseling

One-on-one sessions at our Beaver Falls office. Walk-ins welcome during business hours.

💻

Virtual Counseling

Remote sessions via Zoom or phone for clients who cannot travel to our office.

🎓

Group Workshops

Free community workshops on key business topics — open to all entrepreneurs.

🤝

Cohort Programs

6-week intensive cohorts that combine structured learning with peer accountability.

Ready to Get Started?

Schedule your free initial consultation today. Our counselors are ready to help you build, launch, and grow.
