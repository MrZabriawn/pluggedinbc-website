Revenue Diversification

Government Contracting: Your Next Revenue Stream

Federal, state, and local governments spend billions of dollars annually with small and disadvantaged businesses — but most small business owners don't know how to access those opportunities. Government contracts can provide stable, recurring revenue that transforms your business.

PluggedInBC helps you navigate the registration requirements, certification processes, and bid procedures so you can compete — and win — government contracts at the federal, state, and local level.

Get Procurement Support

Why Government Contracting?

  • Stable, predictable revenue streams
  • Set-aside opportunities for small & disadvantaged businesses
  • Contracts ranging from thousands to millions of dollars
  • Federal, state, county, and municipal opportunities

4 Steps to Your First Government Contract

1
Register on SAM.gov

The System for Award Management is required for all federal contracts. We guide you through the registration process.

2
PA Supplier Portal

Register on Pennsylvania's supplier diversity portal to access state contract opportunities.

3
Get Certified

Obtain MBE, WBE, DBE, or SDB certifications that open set-aside contract opportunities.

4
Identify & Bid

Find contracts that match your capabilities and prepare competitive bids with our counselor support.

Supplier Diversity

Certifications We Help You Obtain

Supplier diversity certifications unlock set-aside contract opportunities at every level of government — and with major corporations.

MBE

Minority Business Enterprise

For minority-owned businesses

State and local certification recognizing businesses at least 51% owned and operated by a minority individual or group. Opens significant set-aside opportunities.

We can help
WBE

Women's Business Enterprise

For women-owned businesses

Certification for businesses at least 51% owned, operated, and controlled by women. Recognized by government agencies and major corporations.

We can help
DBE

Disadvantaged Business Enterprise

For socially and economically disadvantaged owners

Federal certification primarily used in transportation and infrastructure contracting. Required for many DOT-funded projects.

We can help
8(a)

SBA 8(a) — Small Disadvantaged Business

For socially & economically disadvantaged small businesses

The SBA's 8(a) Business Development Program provides access to federal sole-source contracts and competitive set-asides. A powerful tool for business growth.

Funding Your Growth

Accessing Capital for Your Growing Business

Our counselors help you identify the right funding for your stage and needs — then help you apply.

SBA Loans

The Small Business Administration guarantees loans through partner lenders, offering competitive rates and terms for small businesses that may not qualify for conventional financing.

CDFI Lending

Community Development Financial Institutions offer flexible lending designed specifically for underserved businesses. Often more accessible than traditional banks with mission-aligned terms.

PA DCED Programs

Pennsylvania's Department of Community and Economic Development administers several capital programs for small businesses, including low-interest loan programs and equity investments.

Local Bank Partnerships

We have relationships with local lending institutions who understand the Beaver County market and are committed to small business development in our region.

Grants

Federal, state, and foundation grants are available for specific business types, industries, and demographics. We help you identify and apply for grant opportunities you may qualify for.

Not Sure Which Is Right?

A free capital access counseling session helps you identify the best funding source for your stage, needs, and qualifications — before you apply.
