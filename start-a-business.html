Your Roadmap

7 Steps to Launching Your Business

We guide you through every step — from first idea to first client.

1

Validate Your Idea

Is there a market? Who are your customers? What problem do you solve? We help you answer these questions before you invest time or money.

2

Write Your Business Plan

A real business plan isn't just for investors — it's your operating blueprint. We'll help you write one that works for your business and goals.

3

Choose Your Legal Structure

LLC? Sole proprietorship? Corporation? Each has different implications for taxes, liability, and operations. We'll help you choose right for your situation.

4

Register Your Business

File with the PA Department of State, get your EIN from the IRS, register for state taxes, and obtain any required local licenses. We walk you through it all.

5

Open a Business Bank Account

Separate your business and personal finances from day one. We'll walk you through what you need and which account types make sense for your business.

6

Build Your Brand & Online Presence

Name, logo, website, social media — your brand is your first impression. We'll help you build it right, on a budget that works for where you are.

7

Launch & Get Your First Client

Marketing strategy, outreach plan, and sales basics — we help you go from open to operating with a clear plan to land that first paying customer.

What You'll Have by the End

  • A registered business entity
  • An EIN from the IRS
  • A professional business plan
  • A business bank account
  • A basic brand and online presence
  • A strategy to get your first client
